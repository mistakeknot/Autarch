## The discovery

Garden Salon (~/projects/garden-salon, active since 2026-03, deployed at
garden.salon, feel-probes v6–v7 through 2026-08-30) is mk's existing
"workspace where humans and AI agents grow projects together in real time":
Gardens (repo-backed workspaces) + Salons (live sessions around a garden),
CRDT shared state, proposals/annotations as first-class objects, Demarchist
ceremony (cooling periods, silence-is-consent), attribution, succession.
Tonight's "garden salon instead of a factory" was not a new metaphor — it was
mk reconverging on their own articulated philosophy from the Stellaris
direction.

## The scale relationship

- **Garden Salon** is the *inside of one garden*: many participants (human and
  agent) around one artifact, in real time.
- **The Autarch door** is the *estate walk*: one gardener across ~98 gardens,
  asynchronous, terminal-native.
- Same worldview, two zoom levels. Stellaris supplied the estate loop; Garden
  Salon already supplies the room.

## The new piece neither has: guests talking to each other

Garden Salon's discourse is *intra*-garden (many voices, one artifact). The
door's rows are *inter*-garden but mute (98 gardens, no conversation between
them). mk's ruling opens the missing layer: **cross-garden discourse** —
"palettice's pain clause and uncrancher's persona describe the same person";
"this Solwend decision contradicts an IFS ruling" — connections surfacing as
attention-worthy, with the interrupt-fatigue guardrails the Stellaris teardown
demands (classed delivery, never popup-spam).

mk's own reference implementation gap: flux-melange fuses *lenses within one
review* but has no *interconnection step across targets* — mk named this
directly. Bead candidate for interflux; and the cross-garden layer is the
estate-scale version of the same missing step.

### Ruling (mk, 2026-08-31 — leaning, re-confirm at CUJ validation)

> "i think autarch is more like the interface for human users to be able to
> work in the garden salon on their projects"

A stronger (d) than any listed option: **the salon is the world; Autarch is
the human's terminal-native interface into it.** Two clients, one substrate —
the web salon is the room-scale multiplayer view, Autarch the estate-scale
HUD, both reading the same shared state. This gives the HUD something to be a
HUD *of*, makes Autarch's statelessness architecturally enforced (state lives
in the substrate), and natively supplies the three organs the standing
threads were scaffolding (orientation = continuous reading; the bus =
participants sharing state; durable memory = trace-as-form). Phasing caveat
held open in autarch-04's ledger: the walk's organs ship against today's
substrate and do not gate on salon adoption.
