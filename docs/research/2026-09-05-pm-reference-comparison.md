The user asked to include “Linear and other project/roadmap/portfolio
management/execution applications”. This broadens the research to the core
workbench and foundation-to-delivery journey. Gmail and Stellaris remain
references for specific interactions; they do not cover the whole product.

Linear was named by the user. The additional products below are analyst-chosen
comparisons in the requested categories, not inferred personal favorites.

[documentation teardown](2026-09-05-linear-teardown.md) in this pass.

The user's subsequent [product-manager ruling](2026-09-05-foundation-sprint-questions.md#governing-direction-the-product-managers-working-environment)

engineering swarms on product, design, strategy, taste, and discernment.
Agents must apply that guidance in future work and challenge it when evidence
warrants. Compare how each mechanism supports that working relationship.

## Recommended research emphasis

Use Linear as the main workbench and execution comparison, with Aha! supplying
the most direct comparison for explicit product foundations. Asana supplies
the portfolio view, and Basecamp supplies a useful challenge to reporting
progress through closed tasks alone. Examine Productboard and Notion for the
narrow mechanisms of evidence provenance and foundation maintenance.

That gives each reference a job in the research. The recommendation does not
combine all their features into an Autarch backlog. None of the source pages
establishes that its product already delivers the whole human-agent learning
and discernment loop the user described.

## One scenario across the references

Use the current Autarch foundation-onboarding effort as the common case:

1. Return after agents have worked and recover what actually changed.
2. Discover that the next proposed slice conflicts with a persona or CUJ.
3. Inspect the relevant source and earlier human ruling; resolve or explicitly
   defer the conflict under the agreed next-slice readiness bar.
4. Review the resulting piece of work and hand execution through Clavain.
5. Return to the workbench to inspect the delivered result and remaining gaps.

This is a proposed comparison scenario, not a newly validated CUJ. It connects
the existing daily walk, canon tending, and session dive to the agreed first
proving journey. It can show where product management reduces uncertainty and
where maintaining the management tool becomes extra work.
