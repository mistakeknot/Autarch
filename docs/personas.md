# Who Autarch serves

The primary persona is an experienced product leader directing interconnected
software and research projects through agents. This is mk's accepted direction
in the [September 9 amendment](decisions/2026-09-09-human-ratification.md).

They choose outcomes, resolve consequential tradeoffs, express taste, and judge
results. Agents can maintain context, organize work and implement within their
rulings, but the person currently carries too much context among projects and
reconstructs decisions after conversations end or change models.

The earlier solo-gardener persona remains the concrete first user and source of
the [tending journeys](cujs/README.md). The new wording broadens the role without
claiming research with a new user population.

The commercial hypothesis is a workbench for people directing several
agent-built projects. Prove usefulness in mk's workflow first. Willingness to
pay remains untested; this is not a validated market or a purchasing forecast.
