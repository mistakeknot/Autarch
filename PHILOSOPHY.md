# Autarch philosophy

Autarch helps a person direct interconnected projects and judge what agents
deliver. It makes intent, evidence, consequential choices and work readable
together. The [mission](MISSION.md) defines the outcome; the
[project card](docs/why.md) records its human authority.

- Recover existing evidence before asking someone to reconstruct their intent.
- Agents propose and challenge assumptions with evidence. The human rules.
  Answers given while exploring are evidence, not ratification.
- Keep canonical guidance in project files. Retain original conversations and
  attributed source records independently of the application installation.
  The graph is a rebuildable projection, never an authority over its sources.
- Resolve decisions affecting the next meaningful outcome and leave other gaps
  visible. A full set of documents is not proof that a project is understood.
- Preserve exact human wording, essential rationale, uncertainty and superseded
  rulings through corrections, model changes and interrupted visits.
- Separate accepting guidance, preparing a reviewed plan, and authorizing
  implementation. Each action has a visible scope and durable evidence.
- Judge quality and guidance retention before optimizing context or token cost.
  Include retrieval, indexing, retries and corrections in the cost.
- Keep the estate overview quiet. Enter a project deliberately; leaving does
  not create an obligation to clear every remaining question.

These principles amend the earlier preference-only storage guardrail as
recorded in the [ownership decision](docs/decisions/2026-09-09-source-and-graph-ownership.md).
They preserve Clavain's policy and execution ownership. The local Mac pilot
does not establish a portfolio-wide graph service, change signing trust, or
combine provider-context approval with recording permission.
