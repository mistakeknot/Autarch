# Where to look when something is wrong

- A card looks stale or missing: reload Home, then check Settings for the project binding. The Map tab is a placeholder.
- A pick did not reach a thread: the card's conversation shows the comments and instructions on it, not whether a wake was delivered. Ask the owner thread or the vizier.
- Home itself seems down: tell the vizier thread; it owns Home's deploy and rollback.

Anything you cannot explain is worth a note on the card or a line to the vizier.
