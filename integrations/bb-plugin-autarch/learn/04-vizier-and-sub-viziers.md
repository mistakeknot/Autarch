# The vizier and the sub-vizier trial

The vizier is the one thread you read. Other threads send it escalations; it files them as cards, rules on delegated items and relays your rulings back.

For a one-week trial, two sub-viziers (infra, and product/launch) take escalations from listed threads, check the evidence and forward the original message to the vizier unchanged. A sub-vizier merges nothing, rules on nothing and files no card for you.
