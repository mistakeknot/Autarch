# What Home is and what it never does

Home is where the estate's questions for you land. Threads that need a decision, an approval or a hands-on step file a card; Home shows the cards and carries your answer back to the thread that asked.

Home rules nothing by itself. It never merges, deploys, publishes or spends. A pick records what you chose and tells the owning thread. The action that follows still goes through its own gate.
