# Your move versus Waiting on you

The number on the Queue tab is "Waiting on you". It counts open decisions that need your pick now: decision cards that are not on hold and not set aside with Later.

It does not count notices, updates that are only for reading, cards you put on hold, or cards you set aside with Later. The held and Later counts are shown beside the number by name, so a different list can be reconciled with it.

The Queue's own tabs split the same cards another way: "Needs you now", "Later", "Blocked on others" (work stuck on someone else) and "Since you left".
